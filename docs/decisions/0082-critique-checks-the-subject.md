## From “Agents (`agents/`) — what an agent *is*”

**A step whose output nothing reads is a bill, not a capability** (#82). #73 withdrew `critique` from the generator's material on the evidence of one run, and that decision was right about the evidence and wrong about what it left: on a run with no slide deck the step made an LLM call per job (6.7k tokens in the measured job, an eighth of the run) for a consumer that does not exist — the generator ignored it, the refine cycle reads the same merged context, and the only destinations were the report's provenance section and the job record. The issue offered three ways out and named removal as the honest one; what shipped is the third, and the difference is *what the step produces*. **It stopped reviewing the work.** "Gaps, questionable assumptions, concrete improvements" is a memo to whoever will redo the job, which is why containing it cost #58 an apparatus and #73 a withdrawal. Pointed at the subject — claims the material does not support, points where its sources disagree, parts of the request it does not cover — it is ordinary subject material with an ordinary consumer, and the caveats land where `GLOBAL_GENERATOR_PROMPT` already asks doubt to land: on the statement it bears on. Three things make that a different proposition from the one #73 falsified: the output is *about the subject*, so a model copying its shape copies statements about the subject; it is **at most 8 short bullets**, so there is no document skeleton to copy; and since #81 the notes behind it are sourced, so "the material does not support this" is a claim about evidence rather than about the model's memory. **A checker needs the claim AND its source**, so `CritiqueCapability` overrides `_material`: `SingleStepCapability`'s first-match rule is a priority chain over restatements of one thing (the analysis, else the notes it came from), which is right for a step reasoning onward and wrong for one checking one against the other — the same distinction `research.GROUNDING` draws against the same base class. Nothing is bounded there, deliberately: both blocks are model-written summaries bounded by their own producers, and the run's large payload (up to 80 000 characters of retrieved pages, #75) never reaches this step. **Two labels became false and changed with it**: `CritiqueCapability.HEADING` (it said "Internal review of the work — not of the subject") and `SlideDeckCapability.MATERIAL`'s third field, which told the deck the block was a review OF THE WORK. What neither stops saying is what the block must never become — the document's voice, a slide of its own. **Measured, not argued**, because this is exactly the ground #73 was burned on. Structural tier 140/140 before and after. The llm tier (gpt-5-nano, `--repeat 3`, 45 runs) went 505/512 → 492/502, and the three checks #73 left as the instrument were: `report_reader_facing` 22/22 → 19/20, `report_answers_request` 21/22 → 19/20, `refusal_is_bare` 2/2 → 3/4. Each degradation is ONE run, so the two cases that produced them were re-run 6× each on both states (12/12 and 12/12, clean on both) and the eight deliverable-producing cases 3× each on both states (450/450 vs 449/450). Over everything measured: `report_reader_facing` 58/58 → 55/56, `report_answers_request` 57/58 → 54/56 — where both after-failures are `plan_printable_one_pager` naming its subject, which also fails on `main` — and the long, template-shaped refusal that `refusal_is_bare` caught had already been observed on `main` without this change (8 311 characters, in the pre-#84 baseline). A 1-in-56 difference is under this instrument's resolution and is reported as such, not as a result. **What to watch**, because it is the one thing that did appear and did not reproduce: a deliverable growing an *Open questions* section. That is the caveats block becoming a section instead of landing on the sentences it bears on, and it is what `report_reader_facing` would show first if this was the wrong call.
